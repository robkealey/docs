## The shapes of "shared"

Teams mean different things by a shared inbox, and they behave differently with add-ins:

- **A true shared mailbox** — a Microsoft 365 shared mailbox opened alongside your own
- **Delegated access** — someone else's mailbox you've been granted
- **A licensed account everyone signs into** — team@ used as a normal account

## Worth considering: Tags as the shared view

Before standing up another shared mailbox, look at [Tags](/using-lanten/tags). A lot of what shared inboxes exist for — "all maintenance in one place", "who's handling complaints" — Tags do inside each person's own inbox: the same email can be `Maintenance` and `Urgent` at once, nobody forwards anything, and the thread history stays whole.

Shared mailbox for one front door, Tags for the sorting — that's the pattern that scales.
