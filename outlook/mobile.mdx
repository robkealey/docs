The Lanten panel isn't available in the Outlook mobile apps yet. **Mobile support is coming** — this page will change when it ships.

## What you still get on your phone

- **Tags follow you.** Tags are Outlook categories, and categories sync to Outlook on iOS and Android — so an inbox triaged by Lanten on desktop is still colour-coded on your phone. `Urgent` is still urgent from the train.
- **Drafts are just drafts.** Anything Lanten drafted and you saved lives in your mailbox like any other draft — you can review and send it from mobile.

## Meanwhile

The panel — records, summaries, linked threads, suggested actions — needs desktop or web Outlook: new or classic on Windows, [outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com) in any browser, or Outlook for Mac. The web app is the practical option on a machine that isn't yours.
