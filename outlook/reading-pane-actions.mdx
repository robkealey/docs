Outlook hides add-ins behind the **Apps** button by default — two clicks and a hunt every time. You can customise the actions shown on each message so Lanten's icon is always right there on the reading pane.

<Frame caption="Without customising, Lanten lives behind the Apps button.">
  ![The Apps button highlighted in the Outlook toolbar](/images/outlook/apps-button.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  This is about the icon on each message. To keep the whole panel open as you move between emails, [pin it](/getting-started/pin-lanten) — the two work well together.
</Note>

## Surface Lanten

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="New Outlook (Windows)">
    1. Click the **Settings** gear (top-right).
    2. Go to **Mail → Customise actions**.
    3. Under the message surface actions, tick **Lanten**.
    4. **Save**. Lanten's icon now appears on every message you read.

  <Tab title="Outlook on the web">
    1. Click the **Settings** gear.
    2. **Mail → Customise actions**.
    3. Tick **Lanten** under the message surface section.
    4. **Save**.

  <Tab title="Classic Outlook (Windows)">
    Classic Outlook shows Lanten on the **ribbon** of every message — no customisation needed. If you don't see it there, the add-in may not be installed for your mailbox: see [Lanten isn't showing](/troubleshooting/add-in-not-showing).
  </Tab>
