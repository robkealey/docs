Lanten pulls tenants, landlords, applicants and properties straight from Street. The connection is made **once per agency**: one person creates the key, and it works for everyone — teammates who join later don't need to do anything.

<Note>
  You'll need access to **Settings → Applications** in Street to create the key. If you can't see that menu, ask whoever administers your Street account.

## Create the key in Street

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings in Street">
    Log in to Street and click **Settings** at the bottom of the left-hand menu.

    <Frame caption="Settings lives at the bottom of Street's navigation.">
      ![The Street dashboard with Settings highlighted](/images/connect-street/street-settings.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Applications">
    Under **Account administration**, click **Applications**.

    <Frame caption="Applications is under Account administration.">
      ![The Street settings menu with Applications highlighted](/images/connect-street/applications.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new application">
    At the bottom of the page, find **Create new application** and fill it in:

    - **Application name:** Lanten
    - **API Token Scope:** Open API
    - Contact name and email: yours

    Then click **Create Application**.

    <Frame caption="Name it Lanten and select the Open API scope.">
      ![Street's Create new application form](/images/connect-street/create-application.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the token">
    Street shows your new access token once. Click **Click to copy**.

    <Warning>
      **Street only shows this token once.** If you leave the page without copying it, you can't retrieve it — you'd need to create a new application and use that token instead.
    </Warning>

    <Frame caption="Copy the token before leaving the page.">
      ![Street showing the newly created access token with a copy button](/images/connect-street/copy-token.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Lanten">
    Back in Lanten — in onboarding, or at [app.lanten.ai](https://app.lanten.ai) → **Integrations** → Street — paste the token and click **Connect Street**.

    <Frame caption="Paste the key and connect.">
      ![Lanten's Add your Street API key screen](/images/quickstart/street-key.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it worked">
    Open an email in Outlook from someone who's in Street — a current tenant is ideal. Their record should appear in the panel within a moment. That's the whole test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing the connection

On [app.lanten.ai](https://app.lanten.ai) → **Integrations**, the Street card is badged **Organisation** — it belongs to your agency, not to you personally. From there you can:

- **Update key** — paste a new token (for example, if the old application was deleted in Street)
- **Disconnect** — stops record matching, suggested actions losing their Street context, and tasks/notes/work orders, for everyone
