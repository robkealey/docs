Connecting Outlook lets Lanten read the email you're looking at, prepare drafts, and apply Tags. Everyone on your team connects their **own** inbox — this step is per person, unlike [Street](/setup/connect-street), which is connected once for the whole agency.

<Note>
  **Lanten never sends an email without your approval.** It prepares drafts; you approve and send, or edit and send. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can disconnect at any time.
</Note>

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    During onboarding, click **Connect Outlook**. Already past onboarding? Go to [app.lanten.ai](https://app.lanten.ai) → **Integrations** → **Connect** next to Outlook.

    <Frame caption="You'll be redirected to Microsoft to sign in securely.">
      ![The Connect Outlook step](/images/quickstart/connect-outlook.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your work email">
    Use the Microsoft 365 account whose inbox you work from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the permissions and click Accept">
    Microsoft shows the exact list — explained below. You'll come straight back to Lanten once you're connected.

    <Frame caption="The Microsoft consent screen for Lanten.">
      ![Microsoft permissions screen listing what Lanten requests](/images/connect-outlook/consent.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each permission is for

{/* TODO: confirm the purpose wording below with Chris before publishing */}

| Microsoft asks for | Why Lanten needs it |
| --- | --- |
| View users' basic profile | To sign you in and show your name in the dashboard |
| Maintain access to data you have given it access to | To stay connected so you don't re-consent every session |
| Read and write access to user mail | To read the email you have open for context and summaries, save reply drafts, and apply Tags to messages |
| Send mail as a user | To send a reply **after you've approved it** — never on its own |
| Read and write user mailbox settings | To create your Tags as Outlook categories, with their colours |

## If you see "Approval required"

Some organisations require an admin to approve new apps. If Microsoft shows an approval-required screen instead of Accept, forward [For IT administrators](/troubleshooting/it-admins) to whoever manages your Microsoft 365 — it has everything they need, including the option to consent on behalf of the whole organisation.

## Disconnecting and reconnecting

In [app.lanten.ai](https://app.lanten.ai) → **Integrations**, the Outlook card shows which mailbox is connected. **Disconnect** stops Lanten reading anything; reconnect any time with the same steps above.
