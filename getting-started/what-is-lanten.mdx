Lanten is an AI email co-pilot for letting agents and property managers. It lives inside Outlook as an add-in: when you open an email, Lanten matches the sender to your Street records and shows who they are, what they rent or own, and what's outstanding — then drafts your next step for you.

Nothing is ever sent without you. Lanten drafts; you approve and send, or edit and send.

## Who it's for

Lettings and property management teams who run their day from Outlook and keep their records in Street. It works with new and classic Outlook on Windows, Outlook on the web, and Outlook for Mac — see [supported versions](/outlook/new-vs-classic).
