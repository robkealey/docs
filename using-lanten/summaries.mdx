Long threads are where time goes to die: forty messages about one deposit dispute, and you need the state of play _now_. The **Context** box at the top of the panel is Lanten's answer.

## How it works

When you open an email, Lanten summarises the thread — drawing on the **thread's history and context from Street**. So it's not just "they emailed about the boiler four times"; it's informed by the tenancy, the property, and what's already logged.

- **Collapsed**, it's two lines — enough to orient you before you read a word of the email.
- **Expand it** for the full summary of how the conversation got here.
