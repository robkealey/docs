Whatever email you're reading, two Street actions are always available in the panel: **Create note** and **Create task**. They're how the conversation in your inbox becomes a record in your CRM — without opening Street.

<Frame caption="Both actions sit under Suggested actions on every email.">
  ![The Create note in Street and Create task in Street buttons in the Lanten panel](/images/tasks-notes/actions-row.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  **First time only:** pick yourself from the **Author** (notes) or **Assignee** (tasks) list — that's how the record is attributed in Street. Lanten remembers your choice and defaults to it from then on.
</Note>

## Create a note in Street

Use it when the email contains something worth keeping on the record: a tenant's new phone number, a landlord agreeing to a spend, a warning sign you want the next person to see.

<Frame caption="Author, entity, contents — and pin it if it should sit at the top of the record.">
  ![The Create note in Street form with author, entity, note contents and pinned toggle](/images/tasks-notes/note-form.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create note in Street">
    The form opens in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    **Entity** is pre-set to whatever the email matched — the tenant, property or landlord the note belongs against in Street; switch it if it belongs somewhere else. Write the **note contents**, and toggle **Pinned** if it should sit at the top of the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create note">
    It appears on that record in Street immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a task in Street

Use it when the email creates a to-do: chase the gas certificate, book the inspection, call the landlord back Friday.

<Frame caption="Assignee, entity, one of your Street task types, and a due date.">
  ![The Create task in Street form with assignee, entity, task type, title, description and due date](/images/tasks-notes/task-form.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create task in Street">
    The form opens in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    **Entity** is pre-set to the record the email matched. Pick a **task type** — the list comes straight from your Street task types — then add a **title**, **description** and **due date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create task">
    The task lands in Street's task list, assigned and dated.
  </Step>
</Steps>
