Tags let you organise the inbox by what emails _are_ — complaints, urgent requests, high-value landlords — without moving anything anywhere. You describe each tag in plain English; the AI checks every incoming email against your descriptions and applies the ones that fit.

Under the hood, tags are **Outlook categories**: they show as colours in your message list, they're filterable, and they sync everywhere your mailbox does — including your phone.

## Creating a Tag

Tags are managed in the dashboard: [app.lanten.ai](https://app.lanten.ai) → **Tags**.

<Frame caption="Name it as you want it to appear in Outlook, pick a colour, then describe when it should apply.">
  ![The Tags page in the Lanten dashboard with the Add a tag form](/images/tags/create.png)
</Frame>

1. **Name** the tag as you want it to appear in Outlook.
2. Pick a **colour** — this becomes the category colour in your inbox.
3. Describe **when it should be applied**, in up to 500 characters.

The description is the whole game. The AI checks it against every incoming email, so be specific about when it _should and shouldn't_ apply — you can reference what the email is about, who sent it (a tenant, a landlord, an applicant), or even the sender's email address.

<Tip>
  Write the description like you'd brief a new starter. The built-in example is the pattern to copy:

  **Complaint** — _"Apply when someone is unhappy with the service, raising anti-social behaviour, or reporting a dispute with a neighbour or another tenant."_
</Tip>

## What gets tagged

- Tags apply to **new email arriving after the tag exists** — Lanten doesn't backfill your history.
- Wrong call? Add or remove the category on any email in Outlook as normal. Corrections don't retrain the AI — if a tag keeps misfiring, **sharpen its description** instead; that's the lever.

## Finding tagged emails

Tagged emails show their colours in the message list. From there, Outlook's own machinery does the work: filter the inbox by category, click a category to see everything carrying it, or save a category search for one-click access.

## Tags vs separate inboxes

The old way to organise a lettings inbox is more inboxes: maintenance@, accounts@, complaints@ — each one a place for things to be forwarded to, lost in, and double-answered from.

Tags are the alternative: **one inbox, many views**. The same email can be `Maintenance` _and_ `Urgent` _and_ `High-value landlord` at once — no forwarding, no "who's got this?", full thread history intact. Ideas that work well:

- **Urgent requests** — anything needing same-day action
- **Complaints** — service issues, disputes, anti-social behaviour
- **High-value landlords** — your top clients, by name or address if you like
- **Compliance** — gas certificates, EICRs, licensing

Running a genuinely shared mailbox as well? See [Shared inboxes](/outlook/shared-inboxes).
