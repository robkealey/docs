For every email you open, Lanten proposes what to do next — drafted and pre-filled from the message plus the sender's Street record. A tenant reporting a leak gets a reply draft and a work order; a solicitor requesting documents gets a reply that already knows which sale they mean.

<Note>
  **Everything is a draft until you say so.** Approve and send, edit and send, or ignore it. Lanten never sends an email or writes to Street on its own.
</Note>

## What Lanten can suggest today

### Reply

A ready-to-send response in context: it knows the tenancy, the property and the history, so the draft isn't generic. Edit anything before it goes.

### Create work order in Street

On maintenance emails, Lanten drafts the work order — property, reported issue, details — ready to push into Street as a job.

### Create task in Street

When the email implies a to-do — chase a certificate, book an inspection — Lanten can suggest it as a Street task, pre-filled. The form is the same one that's [always available](/using-lanten/tasks-and-notes).

### Create note in Street

Worth keeping on record? Lanten suggests logging it against the right person in Street. Also [always available](/using-lanten/tasks-and-notes).
