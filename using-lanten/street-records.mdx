Open an email and Lanten looks the sender up in Street. If they're in your CRM, the panel shows who they are and everything relevant to the conversation — no tab-switching, no searching Street by hand.

## How matching works

Lanten matches on the **sender's email address** against your Street records. When the person is linked to more than one thing — or the email itself makes the context obvious — Lanten helps you narrow it down:

- **Multiple matches:** you get a picker. A solicitor acting on two sales sees "Choose the sale (2)" with both properties listed.
- **Inferred context:** when Lanten works out the property from the thread, it says so — you'll see it labelled "In this thread (assumed)".

## What you'll see

Every record card starts with the person — name, role, contact details — and a **View in Street** button that jumps straight to their record. Below that, the card adapts to who they are:

### Tenants

Tenancy status and dates, rent, landlord, gas safety expiry, last inspection — and any **open maintenance** jobs pulled from Street, so you know about the broken boiler before you've finished reading about it.

<Frame caption="A tenant's card: tenancy, rent, compliance dates and the open maintenance job.">

</Frame>

### Landlords

Their properties, current tenancy status, and compliance at a glance — including what's _missing_, shown as "none recorded".

### Applicants, solicitors and more

Applicants show their applications; solicitors show their firm and the sales they act on.

## When there's no match

If the sender isn't in Street, Lanten still shows the panel — summaries and reply drafting work on any email — it just won't have CRM context to add. If someone _should_ be matching but isn't, check the address they're emailing from is the one on their Street record.
